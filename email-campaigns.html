AQUARIUM EMAIL CAMPAIGNS

Since June 2026 I’ve designed and built the weekly email campaigns and monthly newsletters for Loveland Living Planet Aquarium, the fifth-largest aquarium in the U.S., which welcomes about one million guests a year.

In five months that has meant 40 campaigns and 1.7 million emails: event launches, animal announcements, exhibit openings, monthly newsletters, and post-event surveys. Each one is built in Klaviyo, so the layout has to hold up on a phone and in a desktop inbox.

My goal was to give each event its own personality while keeping every email recognizably the Aquarium’s. Octo-berfest, for example, carries one look from the launch email, through the “know before you go” reminder, to the follow-up survey.

Over those five months the unsubscribe rate fell by about a quarter, and post-event survey emails averaged a 9% click rate.

My role: email design and build.
