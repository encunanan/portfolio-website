CASE STUDY

AQUARIUM EMAIL CAMPAIGNS

THE BRIEF

Loveland Living Planet Aquarium is the fifth-largest aquarium in the U.S. and welcomes about one million guests a year. In June 2026 I began designing and building its weekly email campaigns and monthly newsletters.

WHAT I DID

In five months I designed and built 40 campaigns in Klaviyo: event launches, animal announcements, exhibit openings, monthly newsletters, and post-event surveys. Each layout has to hold up on a phone and in a desktop inbox.

My goal was to give each event its own personality while keeping every email recognizably the Aquarium’s: dark and moody for a 21+ night with the sharks, stripes and confetti for a week of animal birthdays, marigolds for our Day of the Dead ofrenda.

Some of the artwork is mine. Some is adapted from the brand collateral our team creates for each event, so a guest sees the same look in their inbox, online, and at the Aquarium.

RESULTS

40campaigns in five months
1.7Memails sent
¼drop in unsubscribe rate, from 0.41% to 0.31%
9%average click rate on post-event survey emails

MY ROLE

Email design and build, with artwork created by me or adapted from our team’s event branding.
